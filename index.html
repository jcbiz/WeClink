About the club

A social club for light, responsible drinkers.

weclink.club is for people who enjoy the taste and ritual of good drinks—but care even more about the people around the table. We keep things small, intentional, and welcoming.

Why we exist

Bars can be loud and random. At weclink.club, you know you’re meeting people who are here to connect, not to get wasted. Think of it as a living room with better curation.

How it works

We host small gatherings once or twice a week in the San Jose area. Members get priority access, early pricing, and can invite guests when space allows.

Small groups Curated events Member-first

Beyond the drinks

Each event can have a theme—travel, tech, books, photography, hobbies—so people connect over shared interests, not just what’s in their glass.
